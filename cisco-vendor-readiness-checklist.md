# Cisco Vendor / Supplier Readiness Checklist

## Confirm the correct Cisco path

- Supplier/vendor path: for companies supplying goods or services directly to Cisco. Cisco says new supplier requests must be initiated by a Cisco representative.
- Partner path: for companies that want to sell, design, support, or deliver Cisco solutions to customers as a Cisco partner.

## Prepare company identity

- Legal company name exactly as shown on official documents
- Registered address exactly matching official documents
- Tax ID / VAT registration
- Company registration number: 0105554131793
- Established date: October 3, 2011
- Business license or company registration
- Certificate of incorporation or equivalent government registration
- Chamber of Commerce registration, if applicable
- Company profile PDF
- Current certificate files

## Prepare website content

- Clear company overview
- Service categories

- Certifications and supporting documents
- Relevant project examples or industry experience
- Customer references: Kasetsart University, PTT, STE

## Before publishing on Google Sites

- Verify every legal name/address field against registration documents
- Remove confidential customer names or project details unless approved
- Use a professional email alias instead of a personal email
- Check mobile view
- Test all PDF download links
- Publish with a clean URL and share that URL in Cisco communications
