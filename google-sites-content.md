## Notes for Cisco context

Cisco supplier onboarding is normally initiated by a Cisco representative through Cisco Global Procurement Services. Cisco states that new purchase order-based and non-PO-based suppliers are onboarded through Cisco Smart Buy, and suppliers must complete the registration form and onboarding questionnaire there.
